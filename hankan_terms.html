1. The service

Hankan is a study app. You see a sound and a meaning, then write Hangul, kana, kanji, Korean educational hanja, or simplified Chinese into a blank cell. The app does not require an account or sign-in. There is no study server.

  • Review spacing, handwriting recognition, and explanations are study aids. The app does not promise that you will memorize a character.
  • The app does not show stroke-order animation and does not correct spelling for a whole passage.
  • Readings, meanings, and explanations are drafts written for Hankan. They are not an official dictionary or textbook table.
  • Handwriting recognition and read-aloud speech stay on the device. If recognition is unsure, you confirm the result yourself. On Android, a language model may download once if it is missing. That download is a model file and does not contain your handwriting.
  • The Android app shows Google AdMob banners and an interstitial after a finished study session. The writing screen has no ads. In Settings, watching a rewarded ad to the end turns off banners and interstitials for 24 hours. You can study without watching an ad.
  • The iOS app currently has no ads. If that changes, this page will be updated.
  • A tablet or pad with a stylus is recommended. Finger input works, and a stylus-only setting is available.
  • Courses you import from Excel, CSV, or JSON can be studied in the app. That material is yours and stays on the device.
